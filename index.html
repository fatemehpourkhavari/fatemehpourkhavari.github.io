Fatemeh Pourkhavari

Quantum Optics · Entanglement · Quantum Memories

I am a PhD student at the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics, University of Ljubljana, where I work in the Laboratory for Quantum Optics and Quantum Foundations.

My research focuses on quantum optics, particularly the development of entangled photon sources for quantum communication and quantum memory applications. I am also interested in optical systems, light–matter interactions, and experimental and theoretical approaches to photonics.

My work combines theoretical modeling, numerical simulations, and experimental research to develop and study optical systems.
